# Premium desktop furniture provenance

These are original meshes and original procedural texture pixels authored for this repository with Blender 5.0.1 on 2026-09-06. No third-party mesh, product CAD file, photograph, logo or wallpaper is embedded. Repository licensing remains applicable; this note does not relicense the project.

Design references were official Muuto Outline 2-Seater / Workshop Table and Herman Miller upholstered Sayl product photographs. They were downloaded and inspected locally as visual references; the photographs are not distributed in this folder or the game. These are reference-informed original designs, not manufacturer assets or exact branded replicas.

Reproducible source: `companion/diagnostics/desktop_objects/build_premium_props.py`.

The prior Kenney CC0 assets and their License.txt remain unchanged in the parent folder. These premium GLBs do not derive from their geometry.
