# One-time scene ground admission

The default Windows corridor exposed a real footprint change before translation: Cheval at the recorded world foot had a current rest silhouette bottom of 1391.98035 px at yaw 0 and 1392.36121 px at yaw −0.049, beyond the 1392 px workarea. Its full heading envelope reached 1394.66467 px. The physical foot centre projected to 1376.532 px; the reported 1392 px corridor anchor was the silhouette support reference.

`main.normalize_scene_ground_placement(world_foot)` is a candidate calculation only. The scene admission owner decides when to commit it, once, before preparation. Existing safe points return exactly unchanged. It preserves world Y, finds the closest feasible XZ point across monitor workareas, and rejects corrections over 0.25 m. It does not modify the camera, relax bounds, or continuously move a trajectory. The logged Cheval case requires approximately 0.01337 m inward ground displacement.

Each monitor screen edge becomes a linear inequality in ground X/Z through the actual homogeneous camera matrix. Taking the maximum over the cached circumscribed heading hull gives four edge constraints; a near-plane constraint keeps the entire hull in front. Candidate projections onto constraint edges and pairwise intersections solve the nearest point in this convex polygon. The union of monitor solutions selects the nearest admitted result. Bounds retain their existing 0.01 px hull roundoff reserve; the analytic solve adds 0.001 px inward numerical reserve to avoid float32 boundary rejection. Final actual projected bounds must still satisfy exact `Rect2.encloses`.

`probe_scene_ground_normalization.gd` passes 18 cells: three rigs × yaw 0/45/90 × pitch 0/30, scale 0.6. Each admitted point is tested against 181 actual cached-mesh headings, exact unchanged Y, safe-point idempotence, and a smaller correction-budget rejection. `scene-ground-normalization.json` records results and helper hash. `scene-ground-normalization-boundary-development.json` preserves the two pre-reserve numerical failures. This helper test does not establish production policy invocation or Windows appearance; those belong to the scene admission/default-idle integration probe.

Independent review requested invalid-input hardening before camera access. The helper now rejects null/detached cameras, invalid viewport size or camera matrices, nonfinite desktop origins, and malformed/nonfinite/empty workarea rectangles. `probe_scene_ground_guards.gd` passes 11 checks, including Rect2i acceptance and an empty monitor list rejection. The 18 geometry cells were rerun unchanged after these guards; both reports record the final helper hash.
